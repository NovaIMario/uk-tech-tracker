Where the roles come from

Every two hours, a script reads each employer's public j*b board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workday). It keeps roles based in the UK whose titles mark them as internships, placements, spring weeks and insight programmes, graduate or entry-level roles, or apprenticeships, and drops anything senior. Roles that come down are marked closed and disappear three weeks later.

It sorts by j*b title and location text alone, so it can miss a role with an unusual title, and now and then include one that isn't really entry level. The employer's own page is always the final word on eligibility and deadlines.
